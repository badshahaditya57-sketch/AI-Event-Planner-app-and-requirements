🎉 GenAI Celebration & Event Planner

📌 Overview

Organizing a memorable event—whether it is an intimate birthday party, a grand wedding, or a large-scale community gathering—requires meticulous planning, budgeting, and coordination. The GenAI Celebration & Event Planner is an intelligent web application designed to eliminate the stress of event management. By leveraging the advanced reasoning capabilities of Google's Gemini Large Language Model, this platform provides users with instant, comprehensive, and highly personalized event plans tailored to their specific requirements, guest count, and budget constraints.

🚀 Features

Intelligent Budgeting: Automatically generates a realistic budget breakdown (venue, catering, decorations, etc.) based on your total available funds.

Dynamic Preparation Timelines: Creates a step-by-step timeline leading up to the event, ensuring no critical tasks are missed along the way.

Comprehensive Vendor Checklists: Suggests exactly what types of vendors you need to hire and what questions to ask them.

Personalized Theme & Idea Generation: Processes your custom prompts and special requests to suggest unique, creative themes and activities that will make your event stand out.

User-Friendly Interface: Built on Streamlit, providing a clean, responsive, and intuitive UI that works flawlessly across desktop and mobile devices.

🛠️ Technology Stack

Frontend & Backend Framework: Streamlit

Artificial Intelligence: Google Generative AI (Gemini 1.5 Flash Model)

Programming Language: Python 3

Deployment: Streamlit Community Cloud
